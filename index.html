Questions

Straight answers.

Do my customers ever see "Slabline"?

No. Estimates, invoices, emails and the approval page carry your company name, logo and colors.

How long does setup take, and what do you need from me?

About a week. Send your logo, your typical costs (ready-mix, base, rebar, labor, tear-out, pump) and two or three past quotes. We build the rest and walk you through it.

I already use Jobber / a spreadsheet / paper. Why switch?

You may not need to. On the call we look at how you quote today. If what you have works, I'll say so. Slabline is built around concrete jobs: satellite measuring, $/sq ft and cu yd math, tear-out and pump access, and options homeowners can pick.

Do I still need to visit the site?

For the pour, yes. Satellite measuring gets the homeowner a real price fast. You still check grade, access and base before you pour.

What does it cost?

It depends on your crew size and what you need set up. I'll give you a straight number on the call, and the setup is covered by the 7-day guarantee.

Where are you based?

I work with US contractors remotely and keep calls on US hours. Everything runs in the browser, on your phone and computer.
